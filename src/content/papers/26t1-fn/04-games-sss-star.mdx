## How the keyed sets arise

SSS\* processes (node, bound) PROBLEM entries until the root resolves:

- **SOLVED**: leaves whose exact evaluation entered the root's proof.
- **Queue-purged**: solved entries evicted once a MAX ancestor above them resolved, making them redundant for future expansion.

With Root MIN selecting its smallest MAX child, the proof consumes exactly one witness leaf per MAX circle plus the full leaf-set of the winning circle:

| Proof role | Leaves |
|---|---|
| Witnesses capping losing MAX circles (value ≤ live bound) | **A, E** (+ siblings dismissed unread) |
| Full resolution of the winning branch | **F, G** |

Hence SOLVED $= \{A, E, F, G\}$; of those, the witness-only entries **A, E, G** are purged when their MAX ancestors lock in (the question's 4.2 mechanism), while the winning branch's leaves stay load-bearing.

The general recipe — worth reproducing even where the printed leaf values differ from this transcription:

1. Run minimax: identify the winning MAX circle under the MIN root.
2. SOLVED = winning circle's full leaf set ∪ cheapest witness from every losing circle.
3. Dismissed-forever = dominated siblings never read.
4. Purged = witnesses whose queue entries outlived their usefulness.

## Tricks & Tips

<Accordion title="Exam shortcuts">
1. Minimax first - SSS* returns the identical root value and the backup table reveals which circle carries the proof.
2. Witness economy: each LOSING MAX circle contributes exactly ONE solved leaf (its cheapest); the WINNING circle contributes ALL its leaves.
3. 'Pruned from the queue' ≠ 'dismissed': purged nodes WERE solved; dismissed nodes were never read. Keep the three sets disjoint in your head.
4. Tie-breakers only matter among equal bounds - resolve by deepest-then-leftmost exactly as stated.
</Accordion>
