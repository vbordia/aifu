## The Topic in 60 Seconds

Each junction must realize one legal dictionary pattern; shared edges agree at both ends. Empty domain ⇒ NIL — but not every drawing is inconsistent!

> Deep-dive: [Waltz Line Labeling](/concepts/week-11-constraint-satisfaction/02-waltz-line-labeling).

## 7.1 — A consistent labeling exists → `L4, L2, W2, T3`

The official answer labels junctions 1, 2, 3, 4 as **L4, L2, W2, T3**:

- **Junction 1 = L4**: both edges are arrows pointing *away* from the junction.
- **Junction 2 = L2**: one arrow away + one concave (−).
- **Junction 3 = W2**: minus on one diagonal, + on the stem, minus on the other diagonal.
- **Junction 4 = T3**: bar arrows outward, **+** on the stem.

The verification recipe:

1. **Start at the T (junction 4)**: its bar arrows are forced outward, pinning two edges immediately; the stem carries **+**.
2. Propagate each pinned edge to its neighbour junction and strike out dictionary patterns that no longer fit.
3. Every junction retains exactly one pattern — the assignment is consistent, so the answer is the label list itself (no NIL).

When a drawing *does* label consistently, the answer is the junction labels in the requested order — here **L4, L2, W2, T3** ✓ (the paper also accepted the mirrored reading L6, L6, W1, T3 for the rotated drawing).

## 7.2 — The second drawing → `NIL`

The second drawing's propagation dead-ends: after forcing its most constrained junction, one junction is left demanding an edge label (an arrow direction or +/− pair) that no dictionary pattern provides — its domain empties → **NIL** ✓

The method is identical: classify → force the T/boundary constraints → propagate → first empty domain ⇒ NIL.

## Tricks & Tips

<Accordion title="The 4-step Waltz exam method">
1. Classify every junction (2 edges = L; collinear pair = T; 3-edge fan = W; ~120° trio = Y).
2. Force the most constrained junction first (T bars; boundary-edge arrows forcing W1).
3. Propagate; keep striking patterns.
4. Empty domain ⇒ NIL; a fully consistent assignment ⇒ list the junction labels in the requested order.
</Accordion>

**Answers:** 7.1 `L4,L2,W2,T3` · 7.2 `NIL`
